Paid Social · Full-Funnel Conversion 2026

E-Commerce · Baby Care & Toiletries — 7.27x ROAS Across a Full-Funnel Meta Strategy

Ran the Meta Ads program for an e-commerce retailer's baby care and toiletries product lines, pairing always-on awareness campaigns with sale-timed conversion pushes.

  • 7.27xROAS on conversion spend
  • 6,683Purchases driven
  • ₨504Cost per purchase

Challenge — This e-commerce retailer needed its baby care and toiletries product lines to convert consistently on Meta, without top-of-funnel awareness spend competing with the budget set aside for direct sales campaigns.

Strategy — Split the account into two deliberate tracks: a continuous awareness/reach layer (video, reel, and reach campaigns) that built qualified audience pools and retargeting lists, and a conversion layer timed to key retail moments — Winter Sale, 12.12, New Year Sale, Ramadan Sale, Mother's Day, and ongoing monthly offers.

Execution — Tested creative and audience variants across each sale window, built warm-audience lookalikes off the awareness layer to feed conversion campaigns, and kept an always-on page engagement program running in parallel to compound organic reach between sale cycles.

Result — Over the last 6 months, conversion campaigns spent ₨3.37M and returned ₨24.46M in tracked purchase value — a 7.27x ROAS at a ₨504 average cost per purchase — while the awareness layer delivered 83M+ in cumulative reach to keep the funnel fed. The most recent campaign, a July 25%-off promotion, is outperforming the historical average at 19.07x ROAS.

Paid Social · Seasonal Conversion 2026

Food & Beverage · Rice & Cooking Mixes — 17.65x ROAS Riding the Ramadan & Eid Calendar

Ran Meta conversion campaigns for a rice, spice, and cooking-mix food brand, timing discount pushes to Ramadan and Eid while keeping the audience warm with recipe content in between.

  • 17.65xROAS on conversion spend
  • 786Purchases driven
  • ₨460Cost per purchase

Challenge — This food brand needed its rice, spice, and cooking-mix products to convert consistently on Meta year-round, not just during one-off promotional bursts, while keeping cost per purchase sustainable in a price-sensitive grocery category.

Strategy — Anchored the media calendar around Pakistan's two highest-intent cooking moments — Ramadan and Eid — running dedicated discount conversion campaigns timed to each, while keeping an evergreen conversion campaign live in the background and boosting recipe-style content to build appetite and brand recall between sale windows.

Execution — Launched a Ramadan Discount Conversion campaign ahead of the fasting season, followed by two Eid Feast discount pushes timed to the Eid shopping windows, while running lightweight post-engagement boosts on recipe content to keep the audience warm at low cost.

Result — Conversion campaigns spent ₨361K and returned ₨6.38M in tracked purchase value — a 17.65x ROAS at ₨460 per purchase — with the Eid Feast Discount campaign alone hitting 40.12x ROAS, the single best-performing campaign across the account.

Paid Social · Brand Awareness & Content 2026

Electrical Goods & Lighting — 10.4M Video Views at ₨0.14 Each

Ran a year-round brand content calendar for an electrical goods and home lighting brand on Meta, pairing a PSL cricket sponsorship series and Ramadan creative with always-on engagement content.

  • 10.4MVideo views
  • 710KPost engagements
  • ₨49Blended CPM

Challenge — This electrical goods brand needed a content engine that could deliver awareness and engagement at scale on Meta, without a purchase-tracked funnel to lean on for justifying spend.

Strategy — Built a year-round content calendar anchored to the moments this brand's audience actually pays attention to: a Ramadan dynamic video creative push, an official PSL (Pakistan Super League) cricket sponsorship content series, and always-on page engagement and awareness campaigns to fill the gaps in between.

Execution — Produced and tested multiple video variants for the Ramadan push across ThruPlay and 2-second view objectives in parallel, rolled out a dedicated PSL collab content series timed to the tournament, and kept lightweight page-post engagement and page-likes campaigns running continuously to sustain community growth.

Result — Over 6 months the account delivered 37.3M impressions and 16.5M reach on ₨1.84M in spend — including 10.4M video views at ₨0.14 each and 710K post engagements at ₨0.33 each, at a blended CPM of ₨49.

App Installs · Google Ads 2026

EdTech · AI Knowledge App — Scaling to a 50K Install Cap at ₨17.84 Each

Running an ongoing Google App campaign for an AI-powered knowledge app on Android, scaling from a small test campaign into an always-on install-volume engine toward a 50,000-install cap.

  • 50KInstall cap (campaign live)
  • ₨17.84Cost per install
  • 16%Click-through rate

Challenge — This AI-powered knowledge app needed to drive install volume affordably on Android, without an existing user base or brand recognition to lean on, in a category where paid install costs can spiral quickly.

Strategy — Structured the account as a Google App campaign in two stages: a small testing campaign to validate creative and targeting signals before committing real budget, followed by a scaled always-on Android install campaign optimized purely for install volume, letting Google's automated bidding find the highest-intent placements across Search, Display, YouTube, and Discover.

Execution — Fed the campaign a matrix of text, image, and video assets and let Google's asset optimization test combinations automatically, monitored the account's Optimisation Score to keep it near-maximum, and scaled daily budget from ₨3,400 in testing to ₨12,000 once performance validated.

Result — The campaign is still live, running toward a 50,000-install cap — as of the latest report it has delivered 45,538 installs at a blended ₨17.84 cost per install, a 16% CTR, and a 9.32% install conversion rate, holding a 96.69/100 Google Ads Optimisation Score throughout, a sign of a tightly-tuned, efficient account.
