E-Commerce · Meta Ads · 2026

Activewear & Fashion Brand: 17.5x ROAS on Meta Ads

Industry
E-commerce (activewear & fashion)
Platform
Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)
Period
April to October 2026
Budget
₨488K total, ₨428K on sales campaigns
Role
Paid media strategy and buying
Objective
Profitable online sales growth across seasonal collection drops

Kumail Ali Mirza ran Meta Ads for an e-commerce activewear and fashion brand from April to October 2026. Fifteen sales campaigns spent ₨428K and returned ₨7.49M in tracked purchase value, a 17.5x return on ad spend (ROAS) across 804 purchases at ₨533 each.

Results at a glance

  • 17.5xROAS on sales spend
  • ₨7.49MTracked purchase value
  • 804Purchases
  • ₨533Cost per purchase
  • 44.6xBest single campaign
  • 846KPeople reached

Source: Meta Ads Manager, 1 April to 13 October 2026. ROAS is tracked purchase value divided by ad spend.

Campaign leaderboard

ROAS by sales campaign with ₨10K+ spend

  1. Sep · Story series · Carousel44.6x ROAS₨18.3K spend
  2. Sep · Story series · Video38.0x ROAS₨36.2K spend
  3. May · Summer · All products20.6x ROAS₨16.6K spend
  4. Jul–Aug · Creator collab (scaled)17.1x ROAS₨67.4K spend
  5. Apr–May · Catalog sales16.4x ROAS₨53.9K spend
  6. Jun · Summer Ready · Men & women14.2x ROAS₨26.5K spend
  7. Jul · Activewear haul11.7x ROAS₨115.4K spend
  8. Jun · Men’s sales · 2 weeks6.7x ROAS₨29.5K spend

Strategy map

Funnel stageObjectiveWhat was runResult
AwarenessBring new shoppers into the funnelReach campaigns around new arrivals, capris and dark-post creative (₨19.3K)846K people reached at ₨22.8 per 1,000
EngagementWarm audiences before the salePost-engagement and profile-visit campaigns (₨34.8K)15.1K post engagements and 3.3K profile visits
ConversionTurn warm audiences into purchases at a scalable cost15 sales campaigns timed to collection drops, a catalog campaign and a creator collaboration₨7.49M tracked value, 17.5x ROAS, 804 purchases at ₨533

Execution

Creative and audience changed with each drop: a spring new collection, summer, a creator collaboration and a September storytelling series. The September concept was tested as video and as carousel, the creator-led campaign was duplicated and scaled once it proved out, and a men’s-only two-week push served as a benchmark for where budget should go.

Key learnings

  • Carousel beat video on the same concept: 44.6x on ₨18.3K versus 38.0x on ₨36.2K in the September story series.
  • Creator-led sales scaled: the creator collaboration held 17.1x after ₨67.4K of spend.
  • Audience-specific pushes need their own creative: the men’s-only two-week campaign returned 6.7x, against 14.2x for the mixed men and women summer campaign.

Kumail Ali Mirza ran paid media for a diapers and baby-care group across Meta, TikTok and YouTube in 2026. Meta sales campaigns spent ₨3.15M and returned ₨33.25M in tracked purchase value, a 10.6x ROAS across 9,140 purchases at ₨344 each, while Meta and TikTok together served 283M impressions.

Strategy map

Funnel stageObjectiveWhat was runResult
AwarenessBuild recall at national scaleMeta reach campaigns in waves from December to February and again in June, plus celebrity-led 5-second and 15-second city-targeted video on TikTok72.6M cumulative Meta reach on ₨4.0M; 88.7M TikTok impressions on ₨3.43M
ConsiderationEarn attention and trust before the offerMeta ThruPlay campaigns around Mother’s Day, Father’s Day and August, a September program with seven creators, and short-form YouTube video8.2M ThruPlays at about ₨0.40 each; 3.5M YouTube views
ConversionTurn attention into purchases around offers26 Meta sales campaigns timed to Winter Sale, New Year, Ramadan, Mother’s Day and a July 25% offer, plus a TikTok conversion test₨33.25M tracked value, 10.6x ROAS, 9,140 purchases at ₨344; 935 TikTok conversions at ₨275

Execution

Meta ran 26 sales campaigns, including promoted posts, and winners such as the August diaper campaign were duplicated and re-launched. On TikTok, 5-second and 15-second celebrity videos ran city-targeted, with a December conversion test alongside. On Google, the 6-second YouTube video campaigns held in-stream view rates of 60.2% and 95.4%, and a Performance Max campaign delivered 135.7 contact conversions.

Key learnings

  • Discount-led offers beat generic sale pushes: the July 25% offer returned 18.5x, against 9.3x to 10.9x for the Winter, New Year and Ramadan campaigns.
  • Winners are worth extending: the August diaper sales campaign and its continuation returned 13.8x and 14.1x on ₨458K combined.
  • A regional sales push recorded no tracked purchases, so confirming conversion tracking before spending behind regional campaigns is the clear next step.

Strategy map

Funnel stageObjectiveWhat was runResult
AwarenessStay visible in local grocery momentsTwo reach pushes around fair-price shops and Independence Day (₨14.8K)452K people reached at ₨32.9 per 1,000
EngagementKeep the audience warm between salesThree recipe and festive posts boosted for engagement (₨17.0K)112K post engagements at about ₨0.15 each

Execution

Each of the seven conversion campaigns was rebuilt for its sale window, with an always-on rice sales campaign live into October. Between sales, recipe and festive posts were boosted for engagement and two reach pushes kept the brand visible.

Key learnings

  • Small, tightly timed budgets win: the Eid Feast discount returned 40.1x on ₨32.5K and the Ramadan discount 20.8x on ₨54.9K.
  • Scale lowers the return: the two largest budgets, December ’25 at ₨188.8K and Win Fest at ₨163.7K, returned the lowest ROAS at 14.2x and 12.2x.
  • ROAS held at 12x or above in all seven conversion campaigns.

EdTech · Google Ads · 2026

AI Knowledge App: 45,538 Android Installs at ₨17.84 Each on Google Ads

Industry
EdTech (AI-powered knowledge app)
Platform
Google Ads (App campaign, Android)
Period
2026, campaign still live
Budget
Daily budget scaled from ₨3,400 to ₨12,000
Role
Paid media strategy and buying
Objective
Affordable Android install volume toward a 50,000-install cap

Kumail Ali Mirza runs an ongoing Google App campaign for an AI-powered knowledge app on Android. As of the latest report it has delivered 45,538 installs at ₨17.84 each toward a 50,000-install cap, with a 16% click-through rate and a 96.69/100 Optimisation Score.

Results at a glance

  • 45,538Installs to date
  • ₨17.84Cost per install
  • 16%Click-through rate
  • 9.32%Install conversion rate
  • 96.69Optimisation Score
  • 50KInstall cap

Source: Google Ads, latest report while the campaign is live.

Strategy map

Funnel stageObjectiveWhat was runResult
TestValidate creative and targeting before committing budgetA small Google App testing campaign at ₨3,400 per dayCreative and targeting signals validated
ScaleBuild always-on install volumeAn Android install campaign with automated bidding across Search, Display, YouTube and Discover, budget raised to ₨12,000 per day45,538 installs at ₨17.84 each
OptimiseKeep the account efficient as spend growsA matrix of text, image and video assets tested automatically, with the Optimisation Score monitored16% CTR, 9.32% install rate, 96.69/100 score

Execution

The account was structured in two stages: a small test to validate signals, then a scaled always-on campaign optimised purely for install volume, letting Google’s automated bidding find the highest-intent placements.

Key learnings

  • Validate small, then scale: daily budget moved from ₨3,400 to ₨12,000 only after the test performed.
  • Asset variety feeds automation: a matrix of text, image and video assets let Google test combinations automatically.
